    Other devices

    Use VEIL on a laptop, iPhone, Mac, PC or router with a standard VPN app or the one built in. A set-up uses the location picked above, lasts 7 days and does not earn $VEIL. VLESS and Shadowsocks stay hidden until you turn them on above. Import them into Xray, v2rayNG or sing-box. This app's own tunnel stays WireGuard, IKEv2 or the browser proxy.

    What Infinitum can't see
    • The sites and apps you use. Servers keep no traffic or DNS logs. Blocked trackers are only counted, never named.
    • What you send. Your traffic is encrypted from your device to our server, and HTTPS sites stay encrypted beyond it.
    • Who you are. You sign in with Cumulus ID; VEIL keeps only a hash of it, no name, email or phone number. Your IP address is never written to disk.
    • Your Wi-Fi. Network checks and alerts run on your phone. The network's name is never read.
    • Where Tor sends your traffic. With Tor on, our server sees only that you use Tor.

    We keep a session tied to a hash of your Cumulus ID, minutes connected, ad view counts, a hashed device ID (one earning account per device) and, if you link one, your wallet address. Privacy policy
