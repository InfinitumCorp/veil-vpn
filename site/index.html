02 / HOW IT WORKS

Encrypted. Nothing kept.

  • Every app, one tunnel

    On Android, all of your phone's traffic is encrypted. In Chrome, Edge and Firefox, the extension routes the browser.

  • No account

    No email and no sign-up. The app gets an anonymous session. We never store the sites you visit, your DNS queries or your IP address.

  • Honest status

    "Protected" appears only after the server has answered through the tunnel, and is checked again every minute.

  • Kill switch

    If the server drops, traffic is held instead of leaking, and VEIL reconnects on its own.

  • Tor over VPN

    One switch sends your traffic from our server into the Tor network, .onion sites included. Nothing else to install.

  • Trackers blocked

    On by default: ad networks, analytics and data collectors are stopped before they connect, in every app. The app shows how many, today.

  • Unsafe Wi-Fi alerts

    Join Wi-Fi while VEIL is off and your phone tells you, with a stronger warning for open networks. One tap protects you.

  • Ads by location only

    One small ad, chosen by the server location you picked. Never by what you browse.

03 / PROTOCOLS

The protocol that fits your device.

  • WireGuard

    The default on Android: fast, modern and light on battery. The private key is made on your device and never leaves it.

  • IKEv2 / IPsec

    Built into Android, iPhone, Mac and Windows, so no extra app is needed, and it reconnects smoothly when you move between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

  • OpenVPN

    Works almost anywhere, over UDP or TCP. Use it with the OpenVPN app on any computer, phone or router.

  • HTTPS proxy

    What the browser extensions use: the browser's traffic, encrypted to our server over port 443.

To use VEIL on another device, open Other devices in the app and choose a protocol: you get a WireGuard file, an OpenVPN profile or IKEv2 settings, with a login that lasts 7 days.

04 / PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

What Infinitum can't see.

  • The sites and apps you use

    Our servers keep no traffic logs and no DNS logs. Blocked trackers are counted, never named.

  • What you send

    Traffic is encrypted from your device to our server. HTTPS sites stay encrypted all the way, so the contents are unreadable to us.

  • Who you are

    No account, email or phone number. Your IP address is never written to disk.

  • Your Wi-Fi

    Network checks and alerts run on your phone. The network's name is never read, and nothing about it is sent to us.

  • Where Tor takes you

    With Tor over VPN on, our server sees only that you use Tor, not the sites beyond it.

  • What we do keep

    An anonymous session, minutes connected, ad view counts, a hashed device ID and, if you link one, a wallet address. Details
